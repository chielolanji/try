My Dearest Aki 🌻

Hello, my dearest Aki. 💛

Today is our 3rd month of love, mahal ko. I hope this little yellow finds you well and brings a little warmth to your day.

You know why there are two sunflowers instead of three or more? It’s because sunflowers are known to turn toward each other when there isn’t enough sunlight, especially during the darker hours. And somehow, I find that really beautiful. When the night gets darker, instead of facing the darkness alone, they turn toward each other.

That’s what I want these two sunflowers to remind you of, mahal. Even when life gets dark, tiring, or weary, there is someone who will be there for you. Someone who will stay beside you and face those difficult moments with you. And I want to be your other sunflower. 🌻
